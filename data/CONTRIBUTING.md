# Data contribution guidance

Submit data only when you can document its source, collection method, permission or license, language, and any applicable consent. Remove personal identifiers and sensitive location details before submission.

Every record must retain a review status such as `pending`, `needs_revision`, `rejected`, or `approved`. A record being present in a public dataset does not mean it is approved for model training.

Training approval requires three distinct qualified reviewers under the project review protocol, including language and agronomic review and evidence/source review. Never fabricate reviewer identities or approvals.
