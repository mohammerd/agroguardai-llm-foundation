# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and project leaders pledge to make participation in the AgroGuardAI community a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and respect toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback professionally.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Prioritizing the project's integrity and the communities affected by its research.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Trolling, insults, and sustained disruptive conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Misrepresenting research results, fabricating review approvals, or knowingly concealing material safety or data-rights concerns.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional research or engineering environment.

## Scope and Enforcement

This code applies in project repositories, discussions, events, and other spaces where someone is representing AgroGuardAI.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant where appropriate.

To report conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer privately through a contact channel published on the maintainer's GitHub profile: https://github.com/mohammerd. Do not post sensitive incident details in public issues. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as the available information and capacity allow.
