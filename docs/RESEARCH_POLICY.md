# Research and publication policy

AgroGuardAI aims to produce results that can be inspected, challenged, and independently reproduced.

## Claims

- Make no performance, safety, language-coverage, or farmer-impact claim without a linked artifact or documented measurement.
- Distinguish measured results, estimates, hypotheses, and planned work.
- Report per-language performance and failure cases; do not hide weak languages in aggregate scores.
- Publish negative and inconclusive findings when they materially affect interpretation.
- Never describe model outputs as agronomist-approved unless the relevant review actually occurred.

## Evaluation integrity

- Keep development, validation, and final test sets separated.
- Record dataset and benchmark versions before evaluating candidate models.
- Avoid tuning prompts or training on final test examples.
- Document sampling, exclusions, confidence intervals, and reviewer disagreement.
- Use competent native-language and agronomy reviewers for relevant claims.
- Redact private or identifying content from example outputs.

## Agricultural safety

High-impact advice about chemicals, pesticide rates, disease treatment, animal medication, and other potentially harmful actions requires heightened review. Evaluation must test unsupported certainty, missing context, unsafe instructions, and appropriate referral to qualified local experts or authoritative labels.

## Data and licensing

Public availability is not proof of redistribution permission. Preserve sources, rights, consent, and limitations. Never include partner-confidential content or personal farm data without appropriate authorization. Training eligibility is stricter than publication eligibility.

## External review

Welcome independent critique. A review should identify the exact commit, data/model versions, methods examined, limitations, and unresolved concerns. Do not present an external reviewer as an endorser without explicit permission.
